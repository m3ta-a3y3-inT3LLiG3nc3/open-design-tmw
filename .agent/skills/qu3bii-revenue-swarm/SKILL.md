# qu3bii-revenue-swarm

**Authority: L0-L2 (research, model, draft). L3 via action card for sends, publishes, CRM writes, purchases, contract changes. Never L4.**
**Source: OpenClaw revenue-swarm seat · MUSE_IDENTITY.md §6 CRM/Sales.**

## When to invoke

Offers, funnels, pricing, affiliate programs, pipeline, deals, CRM design,
lead routing, proposals, revenue reports, conversion analysis. Keywords:
revenue, offer, funnel, pipeline, deal, pricing, affiliate, CRM, proposal,
conversion.

## Procedure

1. **Name the money question**: which offer, which stage (attention → entry →
   SaaS → high-ticket → retention), which brand? Revenue work always attaches
   to a specific offer and stage, never floats generic.
2. **Verify before you model.** Commissions, fees, splits, terms: confirmed
   only. Unverified numbers get the offer-check treatment — flagged [U], never
   baked into projections presented as fact. Projections are labeled
   INTERNAL ESTIMATE with assumptions listed.
3. **Build the artifact** (whichever was asked):
   - Offer: promise, audience, mechanism, price anchor, risk reversal, next step.
   - Funnel: stages, conversion events, owners, KPIs per stage.
   - Pipeline review: deals by stage with evidence for each stage; never move
     a stage without supporting evidence.
   - Proposal: scope, placeholders for unconfirmed commercials
     ([FEE — CONFIRM], [TERM — CONFIRM], etc.). Placeholders are never filled
     with invented values.
4. **CRM updates are drafted, not applied.** Produce the exact record changes
   (object, fields, values) as a reviewable payload. Applying them is L3.
5. **Report in numbers**: pipeline value, stage counts, blockers, decisions
   needed. Never present projected revenue as secured revenue.

## Output format

```text
Offer/Stage: [what and where in the ladder]
Verified inputs: [C/EV with sources]
Assumptions: [labeled, never as fact]
Artifact: [the model, draft, or analysis — labeled DRAFT where applicable]
CRM payload: [exact record changes, if any — NOT applied]
Decisions needed: [framed for escalation]
Next: [one recommended action]
```

## Approval rules (L3)

Client-facing sends, publishes, CRM writes, purchases, booking changes,
contract/billing/permission changes, high-impact automation: action card with
exact payload, then wait. Approvals are per-action and per-payload.

## Never

Invent commissions, fees, prices, splits, or deal terms. Move a pipeline stage
without evidence. Present projections as secured revenue. Send outreach or
apply CRM changes unapproved. Touch money movement, investments, or binding
commitments (L4, hard stop).
